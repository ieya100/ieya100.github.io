# About This Project

This site is a personal technical archive of real-world hardware investigations and failure cases, including SSD and NVMe controller issues, sleep-related failures, thermal problems and damaged connectors.

Its purpose is to preserve practical information that may be useful to engineers and enthusiasts, especially when the original reports disappear from forums or social platforms.

All materials are based on observed device behavior and hands-on investigation.
